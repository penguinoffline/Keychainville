## Features (Mei Mei)

- 100 mm x 50 mm x 10 mm
- Mei Mei at the front engraved at 1.5 mm
- A Pelmeni figure at the front engraved at 1.5mm and 0.8mm dependent on the edge
- A five stanza poem at the back engraved at 0.8 mm
- A message in Ukrainian at the front engraved at 0.8 mm

## Challenges

- Complex Boolean operations involving hundreds of letters and edges (my own fault with the font)
- Reconstruction of missing letters after cutting
